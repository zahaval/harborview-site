Clarity in lending

Asked, answered.

What separates you from your competition?

Harborview is a true one-stop shop for all commercial real estate capital. Our wide variety of debt, equity and advisory products and services truly separates us from the competition — as does our proprietary mezzanine financing platform.

How do I get higher leverage on my transaction?

It depends on the asset class, but HUD loans offer higher leverage in general, and mezzanine options for an additional 5–20% leverage points are available from our Harborview Funding balance sheet product and our relationship lenders for most asset classes and transaction sizes. For smaller deals — under a $10M mezz check — be prepared for recourse. We always add a creative layer to the structuring, weighing leverage against rate and terms to craft the best loan for your needs.

Will an interest-only period be available, and for how long?

Interest-only is typically given in new construction and substantial rehabilitation as a matter of course. The same can be had on value-add, turnaround or repositioning transactions if the need is demonstrated to the lender — generally 6 to 24 months from a bank, proportionate to the need. CMBS lenders will structure interest-only periods of 2, 3, 5 or even 7 years or longer for projects with a particularly strong credit profile.

How long does a HUD deal really take?

For an A7 refinance, either MAP or LEAN, figure 2 to 4 months including all processing and closing. A healthcare refinance into HUD — a LEAN 232(f) deal — adds about a month due to the diligence, underwriting and third parties involved. A multifamily MAP 223(f) is simpler, with timing similar to an A7. Timing is also affected by HUD office workload, which varies by season — choose a lender with a good HUD relationship, and ask about early rate lock.

How long does it take to close a loan?

It depends on the type of loan — typically 45 to 60 days, with the exception of a HUD execution.

Is it possible to get a non-recourse loan?

Yes. HUD, Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, CMBS and life company loans are all non-recourse, and there are banks that may offer non-recourse in certain cases.

What do you need from us to source an equity partner?

A clear, concise overview of the deal itself; resume, background and completed-deal information on the sponsor; and an underwriting model showing current and proposed financials with equity splits and returns. We have many partners — large and small, with different appetites, target markets and return profiles — eager to see new deals from quality sponsors.

What are your typical fees?

Our fees are market based, and we have never lost a deal based on fees. We offer transparency and clarity in every aspect of your transaction from start to finish, including all fees. Many of our clients tell us their savings from reduced rates, cash-out to investors or better leverage utilization far exceed any deal costs.
